How to read the comparison

Without optimal control, every ground has attraction weight one. Once per simulated second each boat computes its target from its distance to the grounds and their fish stocks, then moves at full throttle toward that target. The optimized boats use a planned schedule of targets and throttles. Both policies follow the same smooth movement law, speed limit and fish depletion law. Catch is the initial fish stock minus the stock still in the water.

β sets the penalty for nearby boats. β = 0 still optimizes harvest and travel. Numerical checks apply to the optimized schedules; they do not establish a global optimum.
